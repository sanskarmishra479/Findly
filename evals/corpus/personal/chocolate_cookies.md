# Chewy chocolate chip cookies

Cream 115 g soft butter with 100 g brown sugar and 50 g white sugar.
Beat in one egg and a teaspoon of vanilla, then mix in 150 g flour and half a teaspoon of baking soda.
Fold in 120 g chocolate chips. Chill the dough for an hour.
Bake spoonfuls at 180 C for 10 to 11 minutes; they firm up while cooling.
