# Kyoto trip, April 2024

Day 1: Fushimi Inari shrine early in the morning before the crowds, walked up the thousands of orange torii gates.
Day 2: Kinkaku-ji, the golden pavilion, then the bamboo grove in Arashiyama.
Day 3: Kiyomizu-dera temple and dinner in Gion. Cherry blossoms were at their peak along the Philosopher's Path.
Stayed in a small ryokan near Kyoto station.
