Ingredients

  • 100 g active starter
  • 375 g water
  • 500 g bread flour
  • 10 g salt

Method

Mix the starter, water and flour and rest for 45 minutes. Add salt, then do four sets of stretch and folds 30 minutes apart.

Shape the dough, proof it in the fridge overnight and bake in a hot Dutch oven at 250 C for 20 minutes with the lid on and 25 minutes without.
