Home network setup notes
Router: TP-Link Archer AX55, installed in the hallway cupboard.
To change settings open 192.168.0.1 in a browser and log in with the admin account.
The 5 GHz network is called MapleHome-5G and the guest network is MapleGuest.
If the internet drops, unplug the router for 30 seconds, then check that the WAN light turns blue.
The fibre modem from the provider must stay in bridge mode.
