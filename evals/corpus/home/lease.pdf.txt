HOUSE RULES
Animals: The Tenant may not keep dogs, cats, birds or any other animals in the unit without prior written consent from the Landlord. Fish in a tank smaller than 40 litres are allowed.
Smoking is not permitted anywhere inside the building or on the balconies.
Quiet hours are from 10 pm to 7 am.

PAYMENTS
Monthly rent is 1,250 dollars, payable on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer.
A late fee of 50 dollars applies after the 10th day of the month.
The security deposit of 2,500 dollars will be returned within 30 days after the Tenant moves out, minus any repair costs.
