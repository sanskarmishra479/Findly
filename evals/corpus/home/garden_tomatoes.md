# Growing tomatoes on the balcony

Cherry tomatoes do well in 20 litre pots with good drainage holes.
Use a mix of compost and potting soil, and water deeply every morning in summer.
Pinch off the side shoots (suckers) so the plant puts its energy into fruit.
Tie the main stem to a bamboo stake as it grows. Feed with a tomato fertiliser every two weeks once the first flowers appear.
Last year the Sungold variety gave the sweetest fruit.
